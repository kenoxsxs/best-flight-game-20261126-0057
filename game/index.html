DUSK RUNNER

DELIVER BEFORE DARK — A COURIER'S RACE WITH THE SUN

You are a courier pilot in a mountain archipelago at sunset. Fly the Kestrel (callsign DUSK-1) and deliver the supplies to the remote outposts before the light is gone. The sun is your timer — the sky darkens as the clock runs out.
